# Excel-Sales-Analytics

Here is the rundown of the financial and business performance reports for AtliQ Hardwares covering the 2019, 2020, and 2021 fiscal years. Here's a quick look at what you'll find in each file:

---

## 📁 Report Breakdown

### 1. Finance Report ([finance_report.pdf](finance_report.pdf))

This report gives you a big-picture look at the company's financial health, broken down by regional sub-zones and global markets.

* **GM% by Quarters (Sub-zone)**: Shows how Gross Margin percentages tracked across different quarters for regions like ANZ, India, North America, and others from 2019 to 2021.
* **Market-wise P&L (FY 2019–2021)**: Breaks down Net Sales, COGS, Gross Margin, and GM% country by country (like Australia, Canada, Germany, India, and the US).
* **P&L Statements by Fiscal Years**: Summarizes the yearly totals and compares performance year-over-year, such as looking at how 2021 stacked up against 2020.

### 2. Quarterly Finance Report ([quarterly_finance_report.pdf](quarterly_finance_report.pdf))

This file gets into the nitty-gritty of monthly and quarterly numbers so you can track revenue and margins closely.

* **FY 2019 Monthly & Quarterly Breakdown**: Breaks down Net Sales, COGS, Gross Margin, and GM% month-by-month from September through August, grouped into Q1–Q4.
* **FY 2020 & FY 2021 Monthly Progression**: Follows the monthly flow of sales and costs to spot seasonal trends.
* **Comparative Growth Analysis**: Compares growth across different years on a month-to-month basis, like matching up months from 2021 against 2020.

### 3. Data Analysis Report ([data_analysis_report_atliq_hardware.pdf](data_analysis_report_atliq_hardware.pdf))

This report dives deep into sales data, looking at products, retail customers, and how well regional targets were met.

* **2021 New Products Performance**: Checks out how much money the new 2021 product lines—like processors and smart devices—brought in.
* **Customer Net Sales Report**: Tracks sales history from 2019 to 2021 and growth rates for big retail partners like Amazon, BestBuy, Costco, Croma, Flipkart, and Walmart.
* **Top & Least Sold Products**: Lists out the best-selling items (like the *AQ Master wired x1 Ms*) compared to the slow-moving inventory.
* **Product Growth & Division Analysis**: Ranks the fastest-growing products from 2020 to 2021 and breaks down sales across different commercial divisions.
* **Target vs. Performance**: Compares actual country sales against what was originally targeted for 2021.
